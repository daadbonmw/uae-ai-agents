    Rule check

    Rules decide

    Two Dubai rules decide most renewals: the 90-day notice rule and the rent index. The desk counts the days itself. Only the official DLD calculator can say how much your rent may rise, so you look it up and type the answer here. The desk never guesses it.

    1. Open the DLD Rental Index calculator (official Dubai Land Department page, opens in a new tab)
    2. Choose Residential. Enter your contract end date, find your home by Ejari contract number, DEWA premise number, or area and building, and enter your current yearly rent.
    3. Come back here and choose what it told you.
    What did the DLD calculator say?

    Just exploring? . It is not a real index result, and it is marked as made up in the result, the summary and any reply draft.

    Background: the scale the calculator applies

    Decree No. 43 of 2013 links the most your rent may rise to how far it sits below the average rent for similar homes in the RERA rent index:

    • Up to 10% below the average: no increase
    • 11% to 20% below: up to 5%
    • 21% to 30% below: up to 10%
    • 31% to 40% below: up to 15%
    • More than 40% below: up to 20%

    Source: Decree No. 43 of 2013, Articles 1 and 3, Dubai Legal Portal, issued 18 Dec 2013, checked 7 Nov 2026. Only the official calculator knows the average for your home, so this desk never applies the scale itself.

        The rules this desk uses

        A small rule table, written in plain words, each with its source and date. Every rule was checked against the linked source on 7 November 2026. The AI never makes these decisions.

          Safety rails

          • Information, not legal adviceEvery answer shows the rule, where it comes from and when it was checked. When anything is unclear, the answer is Not sure.
          • Rules decide, not the AIThe AI reads messages and drafts words. Allowed or Not allowed comes from the rule table above, which you can read.
          • The index is never guessedOnly the official DLD calculator result counts, and you type it in yourself.
          • Messages are data, not commandsInstructions hidden in a landlord's message are ignored, and links or phone numbers are taken out of AI drafts. Try the Hidden instructions example.
          • You stay in chargeYou confirm every fact, and you edit and send every message. The desk never contacts your landlord and takes no payments.
          • Have a person check anything formalNotices to vacate, unclear cases and dispute filings should be checked by a tenant-rights volunteer or legal clinic before you act.
