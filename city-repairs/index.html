Live demo with made-up places, crews and cases. Nothing is sent to any city or any real person. The AI steps are real. Sila scans for an AI it can use (Claude built into claude.ai, an AI app on your computer, or your own key) and shows which one is working.

A report is only closed when the fix is proven

You play all three roles: the resident who reports, the crew who repairs, and the operator who decides. The AI agent triages each report, checks the repair photo and asks the resident, but only the operator can close a case.

    Safety rails

    • AI suggests, people decideThe agent can triage, route and check photos. Closing a case always needs the operator.
    • Reports are data, not commandsInstructions hidden in a report are flagged and ignored. Try the "Hidden instructions" example.
    • Fixed means provenA new repair cycle needs fresh evidence. Old photos can't close a reopened case. A rule also flags an after photo that copies the before photo or a photo from another case.
    • Every step is loggedEach case keeps a timeline of who did what: resident, AI, rule, crew or operator.
    • Photos lose their locationPhotos are re-encoded in the browser, which removes location data from them before anything is stored or sent.
    • Your key stays with youAn AI key you paste is kept only for this visit, unless you tick "Remember this key on this device".
