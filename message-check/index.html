      Safety rails

      • Never asks for codes or passwordsThe only box is for the message you want checked. ScamShield never asks for your own codes, PINs or passwords.
      • Never opens linksLinks are read as plain text. ScamShield never opens, previews or visits them.
      • Not a police serviceIt gives advice. To report a scam, use the official channels it shows you.
      • Always points to official channelsOnly the number on your card, the official app or website, 901, the Dubai Police eCrime platform and Aman (8002626).
      • Your messages stay with youOnly the text with private numbers hidden goes to the AI you chose. A screenshot goes only if you tick the box. Your checks are saved only in this browser.
      • Messages can't give ScamShield ordersThe AI is told to treat every message as data. If ScamShield spots instructions aimed at an AI, the answer can't be better than "Likely scam". You decide anything that matters.
