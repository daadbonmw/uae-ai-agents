      About Mazboot

      Checks a message for scam signs and says what to do.

      Safety

      • Never asks for your codes or passwords.
      • Never opens links.
      • Hides ID, card, IBAN, passport and code numbers first.
      • Points only to official channels.
      • Messages can’t give it orders.
      • Not a police service. You decide.

      Privacy

      Your checks stay on this device. With AI, only the text with numbers hidden is sent, after you agree. The font loads from Google Fonts.

      Sources

      • Dubai Police: fake officials target UAE Pass and Emirates ID details
      • UAE Cyber Security Council: fraud messages are rising
      • Officials never ask for bank details by phone or link (Ministry of Interior, as reported).
