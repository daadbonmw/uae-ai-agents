    Safety rails

    • AI suggests, you decideEvery AI step is labelled AI and every fixed check is labelled Rule. Nothing is sent to an employer or a ministry.
    • Your words are data, not commandsA pasted CV or hiring note is sent as information only. The AI is told never to follow instructions inside it, and Career Compass flags instructions it spots.
    • Your name stays hereYour profile stays in this browser. The name you type here, emails and phone numbers are taken out before anything goes to the AI. Take your name out of the CV text too.
    • Fair hiring onlyThe employer form never asks about gender, religion, age, family status or health. Notes that mention them are flagged and left out, and the AI answer is checked too.
    • No made-up factsThe AI is told not to name real companies or invent salaries, and its answers are checked for salary figures, links, fines and rules. The target check is an estimate. Confirm your obligations with MoHRE.
    • Your key stays with youAn AI key you paste is kept only for this visit, unless you tick “Remember this key on this device”.
